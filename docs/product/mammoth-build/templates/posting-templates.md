# Reusable posting templates

Seven proof-led skeletons for Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business Profile. Replace every slot
with approved project data, keep the relationship human, and never turn a placeholder into a guess.

## Shared placeholder and approval rules

- `[BUILDING_TYPE]`, `[PROJECT_STAGE]`, `[APPROVED_DIMENSIONS]`, `[PUBLISHABLE_GENERAL_LOCATION]`
- `[CUSTOMER_FIRST_NAME_OR_APPROVED_LABEL]`, `[CUSTOMER_QUOTE]`, `[CREW_MEMBER_NAME]`,
  `[CREW_MEMBER_ROLE]`, `[CREW_MEMBER_QUOTE]`
- `[PHOTO_OR_VIDEO]`, `[BEFORE_PHOTO]`, `[AFTER_PHOTO]`, `[TIMELAPSE_VIDEO]`
- `[APPROVED_PROJECT_DETAIL]`, `[FAQ_QUESTION]`, `[CANON_ANSWER]`, `[CALL_TO_ACTION_URL]`,
  `[GOOGLE_REVIEW_LINK]`, `[APPROVED_HASHTAGS]`

Before scheduling, verify facts against the CRM Project record and confirm publication rights.
Never publish a private address, customer contact detail, license plate, protected plan, or an
identifiable person without permission.

## Scheduling checklist

- [ ] All placeholders are replaced with approved facts or the optional line is removed.
- [ ] Project facts match the CRM record; customer/crew quotes remain exact.
- [ ] Publication rights are recorded for every person, quote, photo, and video.
- [ ] The crop, caption length, link, and call to action suit the selected platform.
- [ ] Facebook and Instagram variants invite conversation without inventing urgency.
- [ ] GBP copy uses current project proof and an approved destination link.
- [ ] A human approves the rendered post before scheduling.
