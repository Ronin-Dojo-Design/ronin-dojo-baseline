## 3. Business type — address vs service-area (decision needed)

Google forces a choice at setup: **storefront** (customers come to you; a verified street address
shows on the map) vs **service-area business (SAB)** (you go to the customer; the street address is
hidden and you list served regions instead). This choice is a `[VERIFY]` the operator makes.

- **Recommended default: service-area business.** A regional metal-building builder generally
  serves customers on-site across a footprint rather than receiving walk-ins. SAB hides the street
  address (protects a home/office address) and lets you list service regions (§4).
- If Mammoth has a genuine **public office/yard** customers visit, a storefront listing with the
  verified address is stronger for Map Pack presence there.
- **Never publish a personal home address publicly.** If the eligible address is a residence,
  choose SAB and hide it.

`[VERIFY: storefront vs service-area, and the GBP-eligible address to verify against.]`
